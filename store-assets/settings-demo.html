Showmark

Draw on any web page. Your drawings never leave your browser.

Launch shortcut

On any page, press to start drawing. Press it again to leave.

This shortcut is managed by the browser.

One thing to set up

Chrome couldn't assign a launch shortcut automatically, probably because Alt+Shift+S is already taken.

  1. Click the button below. It opens your browser's extension shortcuts page.
  2. Find Showmark and click the pencil next to "Toggle Showmark on the current page".
  3. Press the key combination you want, then come back to this tab.

Or open chrome://extensions/shortcuts yourself.

Shortcuts while drawing

Click a key to change it, then press the new one. Single letters or digits 7–0 only.

Shift + dragPerfect square or circle
1 – 6Pick a color
EscClear everything
Esc EscExit Showmark (two quick presses)

Usage data

Counts which tools, colors, shortcuts and settings get used, so Showmark improves where it matters. Tied to a random ID, not to you. Never includes page addresses, page content or anything you type. Privacy policy

Tip

Pin Showmark from the puzzle-piece menu in the toolbar so you can also launch it with a click. Drag the drawing toolbar by its handle to move it; it remembers where you left it.

Showmark can't run on browser pages such as chrome:// or the Chrome Web Store.
